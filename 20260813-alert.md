# ALERT: The Proof of Claim deadline is tomorrow — Friday, August 14

*This is a short special alert, not a regular weekly issue. There is no new docket news this week; the next regular issue will follow as usual.*

**If Oxford Street Education / Croft School owes you money and you have not filed a Proof of Claim, tomorrow — Friday, August 14, 2026 — is the deadline.**

This is the one step that protects what you are owed. If you have been meaning to get to it, please do it today or tomorrow.

## What to do

File online through the court's electronic claims site: **[mab.uscourts.gov/claims-e-filing](https://www.mab.uscourts.gov/claims-e-filing)**. You do not need a PACER account, and you do not need a lawyer.

You need three things:

* **The case:** Oxford Street Education, LLC, case number 26-11334, U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Massachusetts
* **The amount** you are owed — a deposit, prepaid tuition, or anything else
* **Any proof** you have — an enrollment agreement, a receipt, a billing statement

I walked through the whole process step by step, including how our own family filled out the form for prepaid tuition, in **[Issue #4](https://buttondown.com/croftbankruptcyupdates/archive/croft-bankruptcy-update-4-july-2-2026/)**. A [blank Form 410](https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/2025-04/b_410_0425-form.pdf) is there if you want to see it first.

## Two things to watch out for

**Do not mail it.** A paper claim counts as filed when the clerk actually receives it, not when you drop it in the mail. With one day left, mail will not arrive in time. Use the electronic site.

**The August 19 Meeting of Creditors does not push this back.** The continued creditors' meeting is next Wednesday, and the September 9 hearing is a few weeks out. Neither one moves the claim deadline. August 14 stands on its own.

## If you are worried about missing it

File anyway, even if it is late — but do not count on it. This is a Chapter 7 case, and the court has very little power to forgive a missed claims deadline. A claim filed after August 14 generally goes to the back of the line, behind everyone who filed on time, and in most cases like this that means it is paid nothing.

The short version: tomorrow is the day.

---

*Nothing in this alert is legal advice. You do not need a lawyer to file a Proof of Claim, but you should talk to your own lawyer if you have questions about your situation.*
